DOMAIN 05

Content Moderation

Professional content moderation operations supporting content review, quality standards, policy compliance, user safety, and responsible digital platform management.

About This Domain

Content moderation involves reviewing digital content according to established platform guidelines, quality standards, and moderation policies. This domain focuses on structured content review, classification, quality checking, and responsible decision-making.

Moderation activities may include reviewing text, images, user-generated content, reports, and other digital material while following defined guidelines and escalation procedures.

What You Will Learn

  • Content moderation fundamentals
  • Understanding moderation guidelines
  • Content review and classification
  • Quality checking procedures
  • Policy-based decision making
  • Identifying policy violations
  • Content escalation procedures
  • Documentation and reporting
  • Maintaining consistency during reviews
  • Professional communication

Career Exposure

This domain can provide practical exposure to digital content review, moderation workflows, quality assurance, policy-based operations, documentation, and professional platform support.

Content Review & Quality Operations

Gain an understanding of how digital platforms manage user-generated content through structured review processes, defined guidelines, quality checks, and escalation workflows.
