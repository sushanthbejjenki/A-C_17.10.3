Digital Marketing

Professional digital marketing services supporting online brand promotion, audience engagement, campaign management, lead generation, and digital business growth.

About This Domain

Digital marketing focuses on promoting businesses, products, and services through digital channels. This domain covers online marketing activities designed to improve visibility, reach target audiences, generate leads, and support customer engagement.

Activities may include social media promotion, digital campaigns, search-based marketing, content promotion, audience research, and monitoring campaign performance.

What You Will Learn

  • Digital marketing fundamentals
  • Social media marketing
  • Online campaign planning
  • Audience research and targeting
  • Lead generation techniques
  • Digital content promotion
  • Campaign monitoring and reporting
  • Customer engagement strategies
  • Brand awareness activities
  • Professional digital communication

Career Exposure

This domain can provide practical exposure to digital marketing operations, social media management, online campaigns, customer engagement, lead generation, and digital performance monitoring.

Digital Marketing & Online Promotion
